⚠️ Scam Warning: Fraudulent Subscription Charges

Some people have been charged for a subscription using the name of Veggie Mania. This is a scam. We do not charge any subscription and have nothing to do with these charges.

What to do if you were charged:

  • Contact your bank or card provider as soon as possible to cancel the payment and request a refund.
  • If you have any information about the scammer (name, email, website, payment details, messages received), please share it with us through our social media channels listed below so we can help stop them.
